## Assumptions

- "Personal contacts" means 1:1 conversations in the imported archives
  (Telegram, VK, e-mail, …), not address-book entries without a dialog —
  without a dialog there is nothing to measure the silence or the form
  of address from.
- The silence is measured from the last message in either direction.
- When the evidence is absent or tied, "вы" is the safer default.
- "Debug mode" in the issue is the same thing as "safe mode"; both
  names are accepted (`--mode=debug` is normalized to `safe`).
